# My Favorite Movie: Spider-Man

> "With great power comes great responsibility." — Uncle Ben

**Spider-Man** is a superhero film centered around *Peter Parker*, a high school student who gains spider-like abilities after being bitten by a genetically modified spider.

---

## Top 3 Spider-Man Movies
1. Spider-Man: Into the Spider-Verse
2. Spider-Man: No Way Home
3. Spider-Man 2

### Why I Love It
- Relatable struggles balancing everyday life and hero duties
- Incredible web-swinging action scenes
- Memorable villains like `Green Goblin` and `Doctor Octopus`
